1. CONCEPT
  - The 4 arms keep the J1-b cross-section: inbound 1 left slip + 4 through + 2 right pockets, outbound 4 (+ the slip receiving lane).
  - LEFT turn (all 4 arms): free-flow slip road, leaves from the LEFT lane before the junction (early exit), own carriageway, merges into the outbound lane with an acceleration lane. No signal, no stop.
  - THROUGH (4) and RIGHT (4): through the open paved area, curved painted guide lines, signal 4 phases (as J1-a). U-turn (4): from the inner right pocket, cars only.
  - The paved area between the 4 slip arcs is OPEN (no island, no kerb inside): it is the star of the sketch. Outside the arcs: corner kerb island or green.
